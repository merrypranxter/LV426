## Timeline Notes and Paradoxes

### The Space Jockey Age Problem
The derelict ship on LV-426 was estimated by Ash to have been transmitting for at minimum thousands of years — possibly tens of thousands. The fossilized state of the Engineer supports extreme age. However, per Alien: Covenant, David engineered the xenomorph in 2094–2104 — only 18–28 years before the Nostromo arrived. This is a significant continuity problem. Leading interpretations:

1. **David refined an existing organism** — proto-xenomorphs existed from Engineer bioweapon research; David engineered the 'perfected' strain; the LV-426 eggs predate David
2. **The timeline inconsistency is intentional** — Scott has not publicly reconciled this
3. **The LV-426 derelict is older** — the Covenant never made it to Origae-6; through unrevealed events, David's cargo reached LV-426 millennia earlier (physically impossible given timeline)

### Amanda Ripley
Amanda Ripley (born ~2111) was 11 years old when her mother left on the Nostromo. When Ripley was recovered in 2179, Amanda had died two years previously at age 66. She lived from ~2111 to ~2177. The Alien: Isolation game (2137) shows her alive — consistent with this timeline.

### Hadley's Hope
The colony was established after 2122 (Alien) and before 2179 (Aliens) — a 57-year window during which Weyland-Yutani knew the xenomorph coordinates and chose to colonize the moon. The timeline of colonization and the specific date Burke directed colonists to the derelict is unspecified.
